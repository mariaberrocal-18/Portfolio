Four things an advisor can show a client.

Paraphrased from Palmarium’s public positioning. Not product documentation.

  • A proposal with its reasoning

    Portfolios are built and rebalanced with the reasoning behind each position delivered alongside the allocation.

  • Risk and backtests in the report

    Reports carry backtests and risk metrics, so a recommendation arrives with its checks.

  • Answers to client questions

    Client inquiries are answered with explanations written for financial professionals.

  • A trail behind every output

    Each output keeps an audit trail, so a portfolio version, its explanation and its results stay in step.

For family offices, independent financial advisors, wealth managers, asset managers, banks and broker-dealers, as Palmarium describes its audience. Read Palmarium’s own description at palmarium.ai.

About this exploration

An independent portfolio exploration of how a financial-intelligence product could let every number show its work. It is not an official Palmarium redesign.

  • Based only on Palmarium’s public product positioning. It does not follow, reproduce or claim to follow Palmarium’s brandbook.
  • The wordmark is plain set text. No Palmarium logo or product screens are used.
  • Every figure is fictional and illustrative. Nothing here is investment advice or a real Palmarium output.
  • The Trace is a concept interaction, not an existing Palmarium feature. No confidential client information is used.
