# Product

**Surface:** personal portfolio for María Berrocal, Senior Product Designer (8 years: EY Consulting → EY Design Studio → Waterplan/Civarea).
**Mode:** Experience (portfolio). The work and the person lead; the interface recedes.
**Audience:** hiring managers, design leads and founders at design-led product companies. They skim fast and decide in under a minute.
**Must communicate quickly:** strong product thinking, high visual craft, effectiveness in fast-moving product teams.
**Constraint:** the work is confidential B2B. No real product screenshots; stories are told with reconstructed UI, diagrams and data visualisation.
**Voice:** plain, confident, first person, sentence case. No design-process clichés.
**Anti-references:** generic gradients, floating 3D, glassmorphism, device mockups, Design Thinking diagrams, UX-report case studies.

> Assumptions (inferred from the brief and the Framer screenshots in `reference/`): case-study body copy is a draft written from card titles; María must verify every claim (see `draft` flag in `src/content.mjs`).
