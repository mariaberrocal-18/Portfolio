# Design

**World:** "Paper & proof": a printed proof sheet. Warm-neutral paper, ink, one sage accent, black-and-white photography.

**Color:** paper `#eeede9` · paper-2 `#e4e3de` · ink `#0c0c0c` · ink-2 `#5a5a56` · sage `#3f7a5c` · night `#0a0a0a` (footer, dark media).
**Type:** Inter Tight (variable) for identity and structure; Instrument Serif for story titles and pull statements. Display ≤ 6rem, tracking ≥ −0.04em.
**Texture:** fixed fine-grain noise over paper, multiplied.
**Layout:** 12-col grid, generous whitespace, asymmetric work grid (7/5, 5/7). Hairlines instead of boxes.
**Motion:** one page-load sequence (hero lines rise, portrait wipes in). Media clip-reveals once. One scroll-driven moment per case study (the evolution wipe). Reduced motion respected.
**Imagery:** B&W portraits multiplied onto paper. Project visuals are reconstructed UI fragments drawn in SVG (`src/visuals.mjs`), cropped tight, never full device mockups.
